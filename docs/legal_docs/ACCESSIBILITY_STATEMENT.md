# Accessibility Statement for SancFund

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026

**SancFund** is committed to ensuring digital accessibility and an inclusive user experience for all individuals, including people with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities.

We continually strive to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards and uphold the accessibility principles established under the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA Title III)**, **Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act**, and the **European Accessibility Act (EAA)**.

---

## 1. Core Accessibility Features Built into the Application

SancFund incorporates multiple accessibility features directly into its visual architecture and user interface:

### A. Dynamic Font Scaling & Legibility
- **Adjustable Text Scaling:** Users can adjust font sizes smoothly from **80% up to 140%** without text clipping, overlapping, or visual truncation (`Settings -> Customization -> Font Scale`).
- **Clear Typographic Hierarchy:** Structured headings, distinct font weights, and generous line spacing ensure easy readability for users with low vision.
- **Operating System Integration:** The application automatically respects your device's system-level text size and accessibility scaling settings.

### B. High-Contrast Visual Modes & Ergonomic Themes
- **Diverse Contrast Presets:** Includes **8 Dark Mode Presets** and **8 Light Mode Presets** engineered for optimal color contrast ratios.
- **Custom Accent Colors:** Users with color vision deficiencies can select custom, high-contrast accent colors for interactive elements, buttons, and status indicators.
- **Adjustable Visual Depth:** Users can adjust surface depth and reduce glare to minimize eye strain and enhance visual comfort.

### C. Screen Reader Compatibility
- **Semantic Labels:** Interactive buttons, input fields, navigation tabs, and status cards are labeled with descriptive semantic text for **TalkBack (Android)** and **VoiceOver (iOS)** screen readers.
- **Alternative Text:** Receipt thumbnails, user avatars, and status icons include descriptive text equivalents for assistive technologies.

### D. Touch Target Sizing & Motor Accessibility
- **Minimum 48x48 dp Targets:** All interactive controls, touchable areas, and navigation icons meet or exceed the recommended **48x48 density-independent pixels** minimum touch target dimensions.
- **Generous Spacing:** Touch targets feature ample spacing to prevent accidental activations.

---

## 2. Conformance Status

SancFund is **partially conformant** with **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**. "Partially conformant" signifies that while the vast majority of application screens and workflows adhere strictly to accessibility standards, certain specialized hardware workflows (such as real-time camera viewfinder alignment during physical document capture) may present inherent visual constraints.

---

## 3. Assistive Technology Compatibility

SancFund is tested for compatibility with:
- **Android TalkBack**
- **iOS VoiceOver**
- **System-Wide Dark / Light Mode Switching**
- **System High Contrast Settings**
- **Hardware Keyboard & Switch Access Navigation**

---

## 4. Alternative Access Methods

To accommodate all users, we provide accessible alternatives for core tasks:
- **Camera Scanning Alternative:** Users who experience difficulty capturing receipts through the live camera viewfinder may import digital invoices directly from their photo gallery or enter expense details manually using structured text input forms.
- **Financial Chart Alternatives:** All spending visualizations and charts are accompanied by clear tabular text summaries.

---

## 5. Ongoing Audits & Continuous Improvement

We integrate accessibility assessments into our development and release process:
- Automated linting for semantic labels and color contrast ratios.
- Manual testing with native screen readers across iOS and Android devices.
- Ongoing review of user feedback to identify and resolve potential accessibility barriers.
